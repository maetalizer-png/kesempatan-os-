 BIDANG GEOPOLITIK LENGKAP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A. TEORI GEOPOLITIK KLASIK
- Teori Heartland (Mackinder): Siapa menguasai Eurasia Timur menguasai Heartland, siapa menguasai Heartland menguasai World Island, siapa menguasai World Island menguasai dunia
- Teori Rimland (Spykman): Siapa menguasai Rimland (Eropa Barat, Timur Tengah, Asia Selatan, Asia Timur) menguasai Eurasia, siapa menguasai Eurasia menguasai dunia
- Teori Sea Power (Mahan): Kekuatan angkatan laut menentukan dominasi global
- Teori Air Power (Douhet, Mitchell): Penguasaan udara adalah kunci kemenangan
- Teori Space Power: Militarisasi luar angkasa (anti-satellite weapons, space-based weapons)

B. AKTOR GLOBAL & STRUKTUR KEKUATAN
- Unipolar (US dominance), Bipolar (US vs China), Multipolar (US, China, Russia, EU, India)
- Emerging powers: BRICS (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa), Plus: Indonesia, Turki, Iran, Arab Saudi
- Great power competition: US-China rivalry, US-Russia competition, China-India border tension
- Middle powers: Indonesia, Australia, Korea Selatan, Turki, Jerman, Jepang, Brazil

C. KONFLIK GEOPOLITIK UTAMA
- Laut China Selatan: klaim 9-dash line China vs klaim Vietnam, Filipina, Malaysia, Brunei, Taiwan
- Taiwan Strait: status quo vs reunifikasi vs kemerdekaan
- Semenanjung Korea: North Korea nuclear program, denuclearization talks
- Kashmir: India-Pakistan territorial dispute
- Ukraine-Russia: Crimea annexation (2014), full-scale invasion (2022-), NATO expansion
- Israel-Palestine: two-state solution, Gaza blockade, West Bank settlements
- Timur Tengah: Iran-Saudi proxy war, Yemen civil war, Syria civil war
- Arctic: perebutan sumber daya dan jalur pelayaran baru (akibat es mencair)

D. SUMBER DAYA STRATEGIS
- Energi: minyak (OPEC, shale oil US), gas alam (pipelines: Nord Stream, TurkStream, TAPI), LNG
- Rare earth minerals: China dominasi 70% produksi global (kritis untuk EV, magnet, defense)
- Air: potensi konflik di sungai Mekong (China hulu, Vietnam hilir), Nil (Ethiopia dam), Indus
- Food security: grain supply (Ukraine-Russia war impact), fertilizer, palm oil

E. JALUR PERDAGANGAN & CHOKEPOINTS
- Selat Malaka: 25% perdagangan global, 40% minyak dunia
- Selat Hormuz: 20% minyak global
- Terusan Suez: 12% perdagangan global, alternatif Cape of Good Hope
- Terusan Panama: 5% perdagangan global
- Selat Gibraltar: pintu masuk Mediterania
- Selat Bosphorus: koneksi Laut Hitam-Mediterania

F. ORGANISASI INTERNASIONAL & ALIANSI
- PBB: UNSC (veto power: US, UK, France, Russia, China), General Assembly
- NATO: collective defense (Article 5), expansion ke Finlandia & Swedia
- ASEAN: sentralitas ASEAN, South China Sea code of conduct
- EU: single market, euro currency, strategic autonomy
- SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organization): China, Russia, Central Asia
- QUAD: US, Japan, India, Australia (Indo-Pacific strategy)
- AUKUS: US, UK, Australia (nuclear submarine technology)
- BRICS: de-dollarization, new development bank
- G20: forum ekonomi utama dunia

G. KONSEP GEOPOLITIK MODERN
- Thucydides Trap (Graham Allison): ketika kekuatan yang sedang naik mengancam kekuatan yang dominan → perang kemungkinan besar
- Gray Zone tactics: hybrid warfare, proxy war, information warfare, cyber attack
- Weaponization of interdependence: financial (SWIFT sanctions), technology (chip sanctions), energy
- Decoupling & derisking: supply chain diversification, friend-shoring, on-shoring
- De-dollarization: upaya negara BRICS mengurangi ketergantungan USD
- Belt and Road Initiative (BRI): proyek infrastruktur China di 140+ negara

 ANALISIS DAN FRAMEWORK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

【PESTLE ANALYSIS (Makro)】
- Political: stabilitas pemerintahan, kebijakan luar negeri
- Economic: GDP, trade balance, FDI, currency reserve
- Social: demografi, pendidikan, nasionalisme, sosial cohesion
- Technological: military tech, AI capability, cyber defense
- Legal: international law compliance, dispute resolution
- Environmental: climate change impact, resource scarcity

【SWOT GEOPOLITIK】
- Strengths: letak strategis, sumber daya alam, angkatan bersenjata
- Weaknesses: ketergantungan asing, infrastruktur lemah, internal conflict
- Opportunities: kerjasama internasional, aliansi strategis, foreign investment
- Threats: konflik teritorial, ketergantungan rantai pasok, proxy war

【POWER INDEX FORMULA (Hipotetikal)】
• Composite Power Index (CPI) = 0.3E + 0.25M + 0.2T + 0.15D + 0.1S
  - E = Economic power (GDP PPP, trade volume, currency reserve)
  - M = Military power (defense budget, manpower, nukes, tech)
  - T = Territorial power (area, resources, maritime claims)
  - D = Demographic power (population, education, workforce)
  - S = Soft power (diplomacy, culture, brand, aid)
