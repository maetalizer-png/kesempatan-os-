═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🧪 KONSEP & PROSES FUNDAMENTAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

【GENETIKA MOLEKULER】
• Central dogma: DNA → RNA → Protein
• Replikasi DNA: semi-konservatif (5'→3', leading & lagging strand)
• Transkripsi: DNA → mRNA (promoter, enhancer, intron, exon, splicing)
• Translasi: mRNA → polipeptida (kodon, antikodon, ribosom, tRNA)
• Hukum Mendel: segregasi (1:2:1), asortasi bebas (9:3:3:1)

【METABOLISME】
• Glikolisis: glukosa → 2 piruvat + 2 ATP + 2 NADH
• Siklus Krebs (Asam Sitrat): piruvat → 2 ATP + 6 NADH + 2 FADH₂ + 4 CO₂
• Rantai transport elektron: NADH/FADH₂ → H₂O + ~34 ATP
• Fotosintesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + cahaya → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

【IMUNITAS】
• Imunitas innate (bawaan): fagosit, natural killer, inflamasi, komplemen
• Imunitas adaptif: sel B (antibodi), sel T (helper, cytotoxic, regulatory)
• Memory response: vaksin → sel memory → respon cepat saat infeksi ulang
